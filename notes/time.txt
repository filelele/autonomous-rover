android capture frame with timestamp since boottime of device in ns.
here I log Frame and Location in milliseconds because:
    - Consecutive Frames has 33ms interval (30FPS), large enough to round up from ns to ms and never get 2 frame at same ms.
    - Same goes for Location, which has even larger latency between updates.
Log use epoch time (not boottime) of system in ms.
H265 Android encoder expect timestamp in us, hence the conversion.
WebRTC RTP(Real-time transport protocol) requires timestamp in 90kHz -> timestamp_us*90000/1000000 or *90/1000.
RTP header size is constrained to uint32_t, hence cannot just send absolute epoch timestamp, must use relative boottime timestamp.
